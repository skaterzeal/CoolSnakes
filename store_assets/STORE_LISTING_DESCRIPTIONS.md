## 4. Full Description (Google Play Store & Apple App Store)
*(Max 4,000 characters — Word count: ~390 words | Character count: ~2,500 characters)*

```text
Step back into the golden era of mobile gaming with Cool Snakes — an authentic homage to the iconic monochrome LCD games of the early 2000s! 

Engineered with 100% original pixel graphics, procedural retro buzzer audio, and a deterministic arcade engine, Cool Snakes delivers pure, unfiltered handheld nostalgia directly to your modern device.

NO GIMMICKS, PURE RETRO ARCADE
There are no endless paywalls, no energy bars, no complicated level progression, and zero advertisements. Just you, the snake, and the food. Grow your snake segment by segment, navigate tight spaces, and challenge yourself to fill the entire grid for the mathematically maximum score of 019960!

KEY FEATURES:

🕹️ Authentic Monochrome LCD Aesthetic
Experience genuine early-2000s handheld display visuals complete with a pixelated display grid, subtle contrast shadows, and four selectable vintage LCD palettes:
• Classic Olive Green
• Pocket Grey
• Warm Amber
• Matrix Cyan

🔢 Classic 1–9 Phone Keypad Controls
Play just like on classic early-2000s mobile phones! Use the authentic 3x3 numeric keypad:
• 2: Move UP
• 4: Move LEFT
• 6: Move RIGHT
• 8: Move DOWN
• 5: Pause / Resume
Prefer modern controls? Seamless swipe gesture controls are always available in Settings.

🧱 Dual Boundary Modes
• Wrap Mode: Glide seamlessly through the display edges to emerge on the opposite side.
• Solid Wall Mode: Test your lightning-fast reflexes against rigid boundaries. One collision with the border wall and the run ends!

🐍 Expressive Pixel Snake Morphology
Watch your snake come to life with a distinct retro head featuring directional snout projection, vintage contrast pixel eyes, articulated body segments, and a tapered tail.

🏆 Local Top 3 High Score Leaderboard
Track your best runs and highest scores. All records are saved securely on your device. Can you master the board and achieve the elusive 019960 perfect run?

🔊 100% Original Procedural Audio & Haptics
Immerse yourself in authentic square-wave buzzer tones, key clicks, food bites, and game-over cues generated procedurally in real time. Full support for tactile haptic feedback on every turn.

⚡ 4 Speed Difficulty Settings
Tailor the challenge to your reflex speed: Slow, Normal, Fast, or Turbo. Speed remains strictly constant throughout each run for fair, skill-based competition.

🔒 100% Offline & Private
• No internet connection required.
• No account registration or logins.
• No analytics trackers or tracking SDKs.
• Absolutely ZERO ads.

Whether you spent hours playing classic snake games under your school desk in 2002 or you are experiencing retro handheld gaming for the very first time, Cool Snakes brings timeless arcade simplicity back to life.

Grab your virtual phone, set your speed, and see how long you can survive!
```
