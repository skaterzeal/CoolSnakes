Privacy Policy

At lokup.io ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the mobile application Cool Snakes (com.coolsnakes.app) available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Cool Snakes is built from the ground up as a self-contained, 100% offline retro arcade game. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or device data from our users.

1. Information We Collect: Zero Data Collection

Cool Snakes adheres to a strict Zero Data Collection policy:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, age, or any personally identifiable information.
  • No User Accounts: The game does not require or support registration, logins, passwords, or social media integration.
  • No Location Tracking: The app never requests, accesses, or tracks GPS coordinates, cellular location, or Wi-Fi geolocation data.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect or track Advertising IDs (Apple IDFA or Google Advertising ID), MAC addresses, or IP addresses.
  • No Sensitive Hardware Access: The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, or storage files.

2. On-Device Local Storage (Non-Personal Preferences)

The application only utilizes standard local device storage (SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iOS) to store non-personal gameplay preferences locally on your physical device:

  • Gameplay Records: Your top 3 local high scores.
  • User Preferences: Audio toggle (ON/OFF), haptic feedback toggle (ON/OFF), boundary mode (Wrap/Solid Border), control type (Keypad/Swipe), and selected LCD color theme.
Important: This data remains strictly on your personal device hardware. It is never uploaded to the cloud, transmitted over the internet, or made available to us or any third parties. Uninstalling the application immediately and permanently removes all locally stored preferences.

3. Third-Party Services, SDKs, and Advertising

  • Zero In-App Ads: Cool Snakes contains no advertisements, ad banners, interstitial ads, or advertising SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads).
  • Zero Analytics: We do not integrate third-party analytics trackers (e.g., Google Analytics for Firebase, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer).
  • Zero Cross-App Tracking: We never track your activity across other applications or websites.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Cool Snakes is a family-friendly retro game intended for players of all ages:

  • COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act - USA): We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. Because our app collects zero personal data, no child’s personal information is ever collected, processed, or shared.
  • GDPR-K (European Union): We do not process personal data belonging to children under 16.

5. Regulatory & App Store Compliance

Apple App Store Compliance

  • App Privacy ("Nutrition Labels"): Cool Snakes qualifies for the "Data Not Collected" designation. No user data is collected or linked to you.
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): The app does not track users and does not prompt for tracking permission.

Google Play Store Compliance

  • Data Safety: Declared as "No user data collected" and "No user data shared with third parties".
  • Families Policy: Fully compliant with Google Play Families Policy requirements.

Global Data Protection Laws

  • GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679): Full compliance with privacy by design and data minimization principles.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California, USA): We do not collect, sell, or share consumer personal information.
  • KVKK (Law No. 6698 - Turkey): No personal data is processed or transferred within the scope of KVKK.

6. Security and Offline Architecture

Because Cool Snakes works completely offline without network communication, your gameplay data is not exposed to remote servers, internet interception, or data breach risks.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect future features or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted here on lokup.io with a revised Effective Date.

Önemli: Bu tercihler tamamen sizin cihazınızın kendi dahili hafızasında barındırılır. Asla internet üzerinden harici bir sunucuya iletilmez, bulut yedeklemelerine aktarılmaz veya şirketimiz dahil üçüncü tarafların erişimine açılmaz.
