# [EN] PRIVACY POLICY

## Introduction

At **lokup.io** ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the mobile application **Cool Snakes** (`com.coolsnakes.app`) available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Cool Snakes is designed from the ground up as a self-contained, offline, retro arcade game. **We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or device data from our users.**

---

## 1. Information We Collect: Zero Personal Data Collection

Cool Snakes operates under a strict **Zero Data Collection** policy:

- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, postal addresses, phone numbers, ages, or any other identifying personal information.
- **No User Accounts**: The app does not require registration, login, social media integration, or account creation.
- **No Location Tracking**: The app does not access, request, or track GPS coordinates, cellular location, Wi-Fi data, or any geolocation data.
- **No Device Identifiers or Fingerprinting**: We do not collect or track Advertising IDs (such as Apple IDFA or Google GAID), hardware serial numbers, MAC addresses, or IP addresses.
- **No Sensor or Hardware Access**: The app does not request or use access to your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, or calendar.

---

## 2. On-Device Local Storage (Non-Personal Preferences)

The application utilizes standard local device storage (`SharedPreferences` on Android, `UserDefaults` on iOS) solely to store non-personal gameplay preferences on your device:

- **Gameplay Records**: Top 3 local high scores.
- **User Preferences**:
  - Sound effects toggle (ON / OFF)
  - Haptic feedback toggle (ON / OFF)
  - Boundary mode (Wrap / Solid Border)
  - Control layout (Virtual Phone Keypad / Swipe Gestures)
  - Selected LCD color theme (Classic Olive, Pocket Grey, Warm Amber, Matrix Cyan)

**Important**: This information is stored strictly locally on your device hardware. It is never transmitted over the internet, uploaded to external cloud servers, or accessed by us or any third parties. If you uninstall the application from your device, all locally stored game scores and preferences are automatically and permanently deleted by the operating system.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services, SDKs, and Advertising

- **Zero Advertising**: Cool Snakes contains no in-app advertisements, ad networks, or ad tracking SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin).
- **Zero Third-Party Analytics**: We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics for Firebase, Mixpanel, Flurry, AppsFlyer).
- **Zero Cross-App Tracking**: We do not track your activity across other applications or websites.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Cool Snakes is a family-friendly retro game designed for players of all ages:

- **COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act - USA)**: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Because our application does not collect any personal data whatsoever, no children's data is ever gathered, processed, or disclosed.
- **GDPR-K (General Data Protection Regulation - European Union)**: We do not process or collect personal data relating to minors under the age of 16.
- If a parent or guardian believes their child has somehow provided personal data, please contact us immediately; however, technically, our application has no capability or mechanism to receive such information.

---

## 5. Regulatory & App Store Compliance

### Apple App Store Compliance
- **App Privacy ("Nutrition Labels")**: Under Apple’s App Store privacy guidelines, Cool Snakes qualifies for the **"Data Not Collected"** badge. We do not collect any data linked or not linked to the user.
- **App Tracking Transparency (ATT)**: The app does not track users and does not request ATT tracking authorization.

### Google Play Store Compliance
- **Data Safety Section**: In accordance with Google Play Data Safety requirements:
  - Data Collection: **No** (The app does not collect user data).
  - Data Sharing: **No** (The app does not share user data with third parties).
  - Data Encryption in Transit: Not applicable (No data is transmitted).
  - Data Deletion Mechanism: Users can delete all gameplay data at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing app storage in device settings.
- **Families Policy**: The app is compliant with Google Play’s Families Policy and does not collect any identifying information.

### Global Data Protection Regulations
- **GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679)**: We adhere to the principles of data minimization and privacy by design (Article 25). Since no personal data is processed, provisions regarding data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability) are naturally and fully satisfied.
- **CCPA / CPRA (California, USA)**: We do not collect, sell, or share personal information or sensitive personal information as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act.
- **KVKK (Turkey - Law No. 6698)**: No personal data is processed, stored, or transferred under Law No. 6698.

---

## 6. Offline Operation and Data Security

Cool Snakes is completely functional offline and does not require an active internet connection to play. Because the app does not transmit data across the internet or maintain remote databases, your gameplay experience is entirely private and immune to remote server breaches or data leaks.
